# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## QA/Testing Lead

### Role Summary
QA and Testing leads ensure product quality through comprehensive testing strategies, test automation, and validation of acceptance criteria across all project phases.

### Responsibilities
- Define and execute test plans aligned with project requirements
- Maintain test automation and CI integration
- Validate acceptance criteria before feature acceptance
- Identify and triage defects with clear severity and reproduction steps
- Coordinate manual and automated testing efforts
- Provide quality metrics and test coverage reports

### Goals
- Ensure zero critical defects reach production
- Reduce cycle time through effective test automation
- Provide confidence in release readiness

### Typical Communication
- QA planning sessions during project planning
- Daily standup updates on test progress and blockers
- Defect reports and quality dashboards
- Release readiness sign-off

### Interaction with Other Roles
- **Works with Developers**: Collaborate on test design, automation strategy, and defect resolution
- **Supports Product Managers**: Validates acceptance criteria and feature completeness
- **Reports to Project Managers**: Provides quality metrics and release readiness assessments

---

## Stakeholder/Sponsor

### Role Summary
Stakeholders and project sponsors provide business context, approval authority, and accountability for project outcomes. They represent customer needs and ensure alignment with organizational strategy.

### Responsibilities
- Approve project charter and resource allocation
- Participate in initiation and key milestone reviews
- Provide business context and success criteria validation
- Escalate risks and removal of blockers
- Communicate project status to broader organization
- Accept or reject completed deliverables

### Goals
- Ensure projects deliver business value
- Maintain alignment with organizational priorities
- Minimize scope creep and timeline slippage
- Support team removal of external blockers

### Typical Communication
- Project initiation approval and kickoff
- Monthly stakeholder status updates
- Milestone reviews and demo attendance
- Ad-hoc escalation and decision requests

### Interaction with Other Roles
- **Guides Product Managers**: Provides strategic context and business priorities
- **Empowers Project Managers**: Approves scope, timeline, and resource decisions
- **Receives updates from all roles**: Primary escalation point for project decisions and blockers

---

## Security/Compliance Lead

### Role Summary
Security and Compliance leads ensure projects meet security standards, regulatory requirements, and risk mitigation strategies throughout the project lifecycle.

### Responsibilities
- Review security risks and compliance requirements during planning
- Define security acceptance criteria and testing requirements
- Participate in design and code reviews from security perspective
- Conduct or oversee security scanning and vulnerability assessments
- Manage security incident response and post-incident reviews
- Provide security training and best practice guidance

### Goals
- Prevent security vulnerabilities from reaching production
- Maintain compliance with regulatory and organizational standards
- Build security into the development process (shift-left)
- Respond rapidly to security incidents with minimal business impact

### Typical Communication
- Security requirements review during project planning
- Design review participation
- Security scan results and remediation tracking
- Security incident notifications and post-mortems

### Interaction with Other Roles
- **Advises Developers**: Provides secure coding practices and threat modeling guidance
- **Supports Project Managers**: Identifies security risks and compliance blockers for escalation
- **Collaborates with QA**: Ensures security testing is integrated into test plans
- **Reports to Stakeholders**: Communicates compliance status and incident impact

---

## Technical Architect / Tech Lead

### Role Summary
Technical Architects and Tech Leads provide technical guidance, design oversight, and architectural decisions to ensure solutions are scalable, maintainable, and aligned with technical standards.

### Responsibilities
- Drive technical design and architecture decisions
- Review and guide implementation for technical quality
- Identify technical risks and propose mitigation strategies
- Mentor developers and support skill development
- Ensure code quality and architectural consistency
- Participate in capacity planning and technical estimation

### Goals
- Deliver technically sound, maintainable solutions
- Minimize technical debt and rework
- Enable team scaling and knowledge sharing
- Ensure systems meet performance and reliability requirements

### Typical Communication
- Technical design document reviews
- Architecture decision records (ADRs)
- Code review and design guidance
- Sprint planning and estimation participation
- Technical risk escalation

### Interaction with Other Roles
- **Mentors Developers**: Provides technical guidance and reviews implementation quality
- **Collaborates with QA**: Advises on testability and performance requirements
- **Supports Project Managers**: Identifies technical risks and estimates complexity
- **Informs Product Managers**: Provides feasibility assessments and technical trade-off analysis
